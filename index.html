AI Screener

AI Screener is a private call-screening service that Rinkesh Patel runs on his own server for his own phone. It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, it reads the phone numbers in his Google Contacts (read-only) so that calls from people he knows skip screening and ring straight through.
