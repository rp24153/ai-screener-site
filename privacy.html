AI Screener privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

AI Screener is a private application used only by its owner, Rinkesh Patel, for his own phone. This policy covers the Google user data it accesses.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's Google Contacts (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly). Of that, it keeps only the phone numbers. Names, emails, addresses, notes, and other contact fields are not stored.

How it is used

The phone numbers are compared with the caller ID of incoming calls, so that calls from the owner's contacts ring through without being screened. They are used for nothing else.

Storage, sharing, and retention

  • The numbers are stored only on the owner's own server, in a file readable only by the service, and are replaced by each daily download.
  • They are not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored numbers are then deleted by removing the file.

AI Screener's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
